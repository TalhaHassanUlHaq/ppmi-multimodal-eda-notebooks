Build reproducible, question-led, publication-grade exploratory analyses of locally available PPMI clinical, biospecimen, genetic and molecular, imaging, digital sensor, and curated data. First establish which files and measurements actually exist, how each record is indexed, and what documentation supports its meaning. A file's existence does not establish that a named biomarker, raw sequence, image volume, or sensor stream is present.

## Scientific method and expected outputs

Each approved analysis starts with a specific research question and eligible population. Report participant and observation denominators, visits/time windows, missingness and structural inapplicability, measurement definition and provenance, quality rules, and the actual grain. Use descriptive summaries and figures matched to the modality. If statistical associations are later approved, state the estimand, repeated-measures handling, confounders, multiplicity, and uncertainty. Exploratory patterns are not causal evidence.

The complete [notebook plan and review contract](notebook_plan.md) defines the seven final notebooks, including the Phase 1 overview and all later modalities. Immediately after its title and brief overview, each modality notebook must show every selected Primary EDA and Supporting source with path, dimensions, grain, and rationale, followed by a complete in-notebook dictionary for **every** selected source or sheet. The dictionary preserves all original headers in order, including unused columns, and records verified description/source, type, documented unit or scoring range, missingness with numerator and denominator, variable role, and EDA use. Unverified definitions are labeled, never invented. Then follow research questions, methods, computed results, figures, interpretation, limitations, and conclusions. Source files remain immutable and participant-level values remain local. The [phase specifications](phase_specs/) contain only currently authorized implementation requirements; later specifications are written after their approval gates and reviewed selection evidence.

## Acceptance and governance

Gemini implements code, outputs, notebooks, and corrections. Codex reviews evidence, statistical correctness, executed notebook presentation and privacy, specifies corrections, and records reverification without editing implementation files. The human researcher decides on selection, cohort definitions, consequential exclusions, major methodological changes, and phase progression. Passing unit tests alone never establishes scientific validity or phase approval. A phase is incomplete until its required notebook passes the notebook contract and the human researcher approves progression.
